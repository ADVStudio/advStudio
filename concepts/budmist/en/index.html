07 — Questions

FAQ

Can I buy an apartment in instalments?

Yes. A 0% plan over 24 months with a 30% down payment. No banks, no income certificates.

Is the price fixed in the contract?

Yes. The price per m² is fixed for the whole instalment period and does not depend on exchange rates.

What land and construction documents do you have?

All permits, land ownership and the construction permit are available at the sales office and on the website.

Is there a shelter?

Every residence has a shelter in the underground car park with backup power and ventilation.

When can I start renovation?

Right after commissioning. Our team can also deliver a turnkey renovation at a fixed price.
