01General

This Policy explains how ADV Development Studio (“ADV”, “we”) processes personal data of website visitors and clients. Data controller: [Full name of sole proprietor or company name], tax ID: [__________], address: [__________], Lviv, Ukraine. Data protection contact: adv.development.studio@gmail.com.

We process data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” No. 2297-VI and, for visitors and clients in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

02Data we collect

  • Request form: name, contact details (phone, Telegram or email), estimated budget, project description.
  • Correspondence: the content of messages you send us by email, messengers or social media.
  • Contract data: details needed to sign and perform a contract and issue invoices.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, request time — logged by our hosting provider and by font and library providers when pages load.

We do not collect special categories of data (health, religious beliefs, etc.) and ask you not to submit them through the form.

03Purposes and legal bases

  • To respond to your request and prepare a proposal — consent and pre-contractual steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), (b)).
  • To sign and perform a contract, keep accounts and reporting — contract and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b), (c)).
  • To keep the website working and secure — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not send marketing emails without separate consent and we never sell your data.

04Retention

  • Requests that did not lead to a contract — up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Contract data — for the term of the contract and 3 years after it ends (limitation period); accounting records — as long as the law requires.
  • Technical logs — per the hosting provider’s settings, usually up to 30 days.

05Who receives data

Data may be received only by providers we need to run the studio, and only to the extent necessary:

  • our website hosting provider (GitHub Pages);
  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Apps Script) — to receive form requests, for correspondence and working files;
  • Telegram — for instant team notifications about new requests;
  • messengers (Viber, WhatsApp) if you chose them for contact;
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs — delivery of fonts and libraries (they receive technical request data);
  • our accountant, bank and tax authorities — as required by law.

Some of these providers may process data outside Ukraine and the EU. In such cases we rely on their standard contractual data protection terms.

06Your rights

Under Art. 8 of Law No. 2297-VI and Art. 15–22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • know what data we process, why, and who receives it;
  • get a copy of your data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing);
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise your rights, write to adv.development.studio@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (ombudsman.gov.ua) or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.

07Security

Access to data is limited to people who need it for work; we use two-factor authentication on work accounts and transmit data only over secure channels (HTTPS, TLS).

08Children

ADV’s website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at people under 16. If you learn that a child has sent us data, let us know and we will delete it.

09Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version is always on this page, with the last update date shown at the top.
