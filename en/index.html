Frequently
asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope. The packages above give a guide; the exact price is calculated after the brief from each specialist’s hours plus a 15–20% buffer, and fixed in the contract.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 2–3 weeks, a corporate website 4–7 weeks, complex projects from 8 weeks. You get a milestone schedule before we start.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. In Business and Custom packages we set up a CMS and train your team to update copy, images, prices and news without a developer.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in Poland, Germany and across the EU, with contracts and invoices in EUR.

What happens after launch?

A warranty period covers bug fixes free of charge. After that you can add technical support, optimisation and ongoing development.

Do you show real case studies?

We are a young studio, so our site shows concept projects clearly labelled as concepts. On a call we’ll walk you through prototypes and our process.
